Privacy Policy

GRE Words · Last updated 18 November 2026

GRE Words is a free vocabulary study app. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains the little data it does keep.

What is stored

  • A random sync code (like K7QF-29XM) that the app creates the first time you open it. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any other personal detail.
  • Your study progress for that code: which words you know, starred words, review dates, quiz and spelling results, and any notes or example sentences you type yourself.

This is kept on the app's server so your progress is saved and can be restored on another device with the same code. A copy is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

Android app only: so that reinstalling the app on the same phone brings your progress back automatically, the app stores a scrambled (one-way hashed) form of the ID Android gives this app on your phone, linked to your sync code. The original ID cannot be recovered from it, and it is not shared with anyone.

What is not collected

  • No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and no device identifiers other than the scrambled one described above.
  • No advertising, analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • Your IP address is used only briefly in the server's memory to block abuse (too many requests at once). It is not saved.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared. It is stored with the app's hosting providers (Render for the server and Neon for the database) only so the app can work.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Your progress and use Reset progress to delete all saved progress for your sync code. To have the sync code itself removed from the server, contact the developer using the email address on the app's Google Play page.

Children

The app is a study tool for students and adults preparing for the GRE. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
